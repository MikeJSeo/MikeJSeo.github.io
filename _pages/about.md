# About

Biostatistician specializing in causal inference, evidence synthesis, and Bayesian methods. I hold a Ph.D. in Biostatistics and Epidemiology and currently work in Real World Evidence, developing innovative statistical methods to integrate and analyze clinical and real-world data. I have contributed to health technology assessments, clinical trial analyses, and personalized medicine research, with several peer-reviewed publications and open-source R packages.
